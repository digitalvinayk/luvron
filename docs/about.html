Our Story

Built by a manufacturer, for the dealer.

Luvron began on a small manufacturing floor in Loni, Ghaziabad in 2022. The brief was simple: build kids tricycles that retailers could stock with confidence — sturdy enough to ride hard, packaged tight enough to survive long-haul transport, priced so the dealer makes a real margin.

Four years on, that floor has expanded to a steady output of 12,000 units a month across 44 SKUs. We now supply over 50 active dealers and distributors in cities from Delhi NCR to Patna, Lucknow to Kanpur — and we're growing.

Our promise to every dealer: we manufacture, we pack, we dispatch in 48 hours. No middlemen, no margin layers, no surprises on the invoice.

— Rajneesh
Founder, Luvron Tricycles
